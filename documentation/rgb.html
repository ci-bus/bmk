RGB lighting

BMK drives a chain of WS2812 addressable LEDs from a single pin, with a fixed colour or one of several animated effects that you change from the keyboard.

Enable the LEDs

In keyboard.h:

#define RGB true
#define RGB_PORT 0
#define RGB_PIN 26
#define RGB_LEDS 15
DefineMeaning
RGBEnables the lighting.
RGB_PORTGPIO port of the data pin: 0 or 1.
RGB_PINPin number of the data pin inside that port.
RGB_LEDSNumber of LEDs in the chain.

The example is the data pin on P0.26 with fifteen LEDs. These four lines are read by the build system, so write them exactly in this form and rebuild with -p always after changing them. See Building.

The LED data is generated with an SPI peripheral whose clock output is assigned to P0.18. Do not use P0.18 for the matrix or for anything else on a keyboard with RGB.

Keys

KeycodeAction
HID_KEY_RGB_TOGGLETurns the LEDs on or off.
HID_KEY_RGB_ONTurns the LEDs on.
HID_KEY_RGB_OFFTurns the LEDs off.
HID_KEY_RGB_LIGHTRaises the brightness by RGB_LIGHT_JUMP. After the maximum it starts again from the minimum.
HID_KEY_RGB_COLORAdvances the colour by RGB_COLOR_JUMP around the colour wheel.
HID_KEY_RGB_SATURATIONRaises the saturation by RGB_SATURATION_JUMP. After the maximum it starts again from the minimum.
HID_KEY_RGB_EFFECTChanges to the next effect. After the last one it goes back to the first.

Brightness, colour and saturation go from 0 to 255. With the default steps, brightness and saturation never reach 0: use HID_KEY_RGB_OFF to switch the LEDs off.

The lighting state is not saved. After a restart the LEDs go back to full brightness and saturation, to the effect given by RGB_EFFECT_ON_STARTUP and to the on or off state given by RGB_ON_STARTUP.

Effects

Each effect has a number. HID_KEY_RGB_EFFECT goes through them in this order:

NumberEffectWhat it doesKeys that affect it
0StaticAll the LEDs show one fixed colour.Colour, saturation, brightness
1Colour cycleAll the LEDs change together through the colour wheel.Saturation, brightness
2RainbowThe colour wheel is spread along the chain and moves through it.Saturation, brightness
3ScannerA bright point travels from one end of the chain to the other and back; the LEDs around it are progressively dimmer.Colour, saturation, brightness

Animated effects advance one step every 60 ms.

Choose the effect at start-up

Put the number of the effect in keyboard.h. Without this line the keyboard starts with the colour cycle:

#define RGB_EFFECT_ON_STARTUP 2

The example starts with the rainbow. Use 0 for a keyboard that starts with a fixed colour.

Leave effects out

Each animated effect can be removed from the firmware, and then HID_KEY_RGB_EFFECT skips it:

#define RGB_EFF_KITT false
DefineEffect
RGB_EFF_COLORS1, colour cycle
RGB_EFF_RAINBOW2, rainbow
RGB_EFF_KITT3, scanner

To remove all the animations at once and keep only the fixed colour, set RGB_EFFECTS to false. In that case HID_KEY_RGB_EFFECT does nothing.

Lighting and power

  • The firmware turns the LEDs off by cutting their supply through the external power pin. This happens when you turn them off with a key and when the keyboard falls asleep.
  • A PCB with LEDs should therefore have POWER_EXT. Without it the firmware only stops sending data, and the LEDs keep showing their last colour.
  • LEDs draw much more current than the rest of the keyboard. On battery, keep them off or dim.
