Tap-hold

A tap-hold key sends a normal key when you tap it and acts as a modifier when you hold it. It lets small keyboards keep arrows or other keys where a modifier lives.

Defining a tap-hold key

Use one of the TH_ macros in the keymap. The macro name is the modifier used when the key is held; the argument is the key sent on a tap:

TH_RSHIFT(HID_KEY_UP)
TH_RCTRL(HID_KEY_RIGHT)
TH_LGUI(HID_KEY_ESC)

The available macros are TH_LCTRL, TH_LSHIFT, TH_LALT, TH_LGUI, TH_RCTRL, TH_RSHIFT, TH_RALT and TH_RGUI. The tap key must be a standard key; media keys and firmware actions cannot be used as the tap action.

How it decides

What you doWhat is sent
Press and release quicklyThe tap key, when the key is released.
Hold longer than TAP_HOLD_PRESS_DELAYThe modifier, until the key is released.
Press another key while the tap-hold key is downThe modifier is applied immediately to that key, without waiting for the delay.
Tap, then press again within TAP_HOLD_RELEASE_DELAY and holdThe tap key held down, so it repeats like a normal key.

The third row is what makes fast typing work: typing Shift + letter with a tap-hold Shift does not require waiting for the hold delay.

The fourth row gives you key repeat for the tap key. For example, with TH_RSHIFT(HID_KEY_UP), tap once and then hold to keep moving up.

Timing options

Set them in keyboard.h:

OptionDefaultMeaning
TAP_HOLD_PRESS_DELAY175Time in ms a key must be held to become a modifier.
TAP_HOLD_RELEASE_DELAY150Time in ms after a tap during which pressing again holds the tap key.
TAP_HOLD_SIZE_ARRAY3How many tap-hold keys can be waiting for their decision at the same time.

If taps are being read as holds, raise TAP_HOLD_PRESS_DELAY. If holding feels slow to react, lower it.
