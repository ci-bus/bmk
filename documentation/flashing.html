Flashing

BMK boards use the Adafruit nRF52 UF2 bootloader: the keyboard shows up as a USB drive and you copy the firmware file onto it.

Flash with UF2

1. Enter the bootloader

Connect the keyboard with a USB cable and use one of these methods:

  • Double-tap the reset button of the board.
  • Press the key mapped to HID_BOOT in your keymap. The firmware restarts straight into the bootloader, so you do not need to reach the reset button.

A USB mass storage drive appears on the computer.

2. Copy the firmware

Drag app/build/zephyr/zephyr.uf2 onto the drive. When the copy finishes, the drive disappears and the keyboard restarts with the new firmware.

The USB dongle is flashed in exactly the same way, with app/build-dongle/zephyr/zephyr.uf2.

Flash with west

The board definitions register two flash runners:

CommandNeeds
west flashA J-Link debug probe and nrfjprog.
west flash -r uf2The bootloader drive mounted on the machine where west runs.

Keys that help while flashing

KeycodeAction
HID_BOOTRestarts into the UF2 bootloader.
HID_RESETRestarts the firmware.

Both are usually placed on a secondary layer so they cannot be pressed by accident. See Keymaps and layers.
